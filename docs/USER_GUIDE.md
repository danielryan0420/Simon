# GEO RACER — How to Play 🌍🚀

## Getting started

1. Open the game. You'll see a spinning Earth floating in space!
2. Tap **Play** on the home screen.
3. Pick a mode:
   - **Countries** — fly to the right country
   - **Capitals** — find a country's capital city
   - **Landmarks** — find famous landmarks like the Eiffel Tower
   - **US States** — find the right US state
4. Pick a difficulty: **Rookie** (easy), **Pilot** (medium), or **Ace**
   (tricky!). You can also pick which part of the world you want questions
   from.
5. Hit **Start**.

## Playing a round

- Read the prompt at the top, like "Fly to: Kenya."
- **Drag** the globe with your finger (or mouse) to spin it around.
- **Pinch** with two fingers (or scroll) to zoom in and out.
- When you find the spot, **tap** it! A little plane will fly there.
- The faster and closer you are, the more points you get:
  - 🟡 **PERFECT** — right on target
  - 🟢 **GREAT** — super close
  - 🔵 **GOOD** — close enough
  - ⚪ **OK** — in the right area
  - 🔴 **MISS** — too far away
- Answer several in a row without missing to build a **combo streak** for
  bonus points — watch for the 🔥 badge!
- After each answer, you'll see the real spot and a fun fact. Tap **Next**
  to keep going.

## Leveling up

- You earn **XP** every round. Level up to unlock new **plane/rocket
  skins** and **globe themes** — change them anytime in **Settings**.

## Daily Challenge 🌟

- One special 10-question challenge every day, the same for everyone who
  plays that day. Come back tomorrow for a new one!

## 2-Player Race 🏁

- Pass the device back and forth. Both players answer the exact same
  questions — whoever scores more wins!

## Learned Map 🗺️

- See which countries you've mastered (answered PERFECT or GREAT) light up
  green on a world map. Try to fill the whole map in!

## Tips

- Tap the 🔊 button in the corner anytime to mute or unmute sounds.
- Your progress, scores, and unlocks are saved right on your device — no
  sign-up needed.
- You can add GEO RACER to your phone's home screen for quick access, and
  it even works without internet once you've opened it the first time!
